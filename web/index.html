IMDC (Heng) risk calculator

Prognostic risk stratification for metastatic renal cell carcinoma. The six adverse factors are assessed at initiation of systemic treatment.

Single-case assessment

Choose Yes or No for each factor. Use appropriate local laboratory reference limits; an absent assessment must not be assumed normal.

Assess all six criteria before calculating.

Risk categories

0 factors: Favorable

1–2 factors: Intermediate

3–6 factors: Poor

This is a prognostic grouping—not tumor staging, an individual survival estimate, emergency triage, or a treatment-selection algorithm.

Batch CSV

Calculate up to 10,000 de-identified records (2 MB maximum) on your device. Include the six factor columns and optional case_id. Accepted values: yes/no, true/false, 1/0.

Your CSV is not uploaded.

Privacy: No sign-in, analytics, server calls, or browser storage. Scoring and CSV operations occur on your device. Do not enter identifiable clinical information.

Evidence: Heng DYC et al. J Clin Oncol. 2009;27:5794–5799. Original IMDC model (PMID: 19826129).
